# board-p1: the network places its own market orders (2026-10-26)

**Plain answer:** with the market head on (`net:board-p1:market`), the network's own orders replace the template's
on days 6-28. Against Metav4 this beats board-v5 (template orders) by **+8,493 margin a game (se 1,421), better on
22 of 24 seeds**, share 1.221 against 1.125, 46 of 48 wins against 40. Better in every scenario and both seats.

## Model

board-v5 plus a market head (28 order tokens x 11 quantity classes, `PLAN_HEADS_DESIGN.md`), fine-tuned jointly
(`--init board-v5 --market --epochs 8 --lr 2e-4`). Training stopped on the last batch of epoch 8 (scheduler step
count; fixed in `tools/train_board_net.py`); the saved model is epoch 7, the best by analysis command accuracy.

| Epoch | Command accuracy, analysis / analysis2 | Market recall, analysis / analysis2 | Market precision, analysis / analysis2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 89.65% / 90.38% | 55.1% / 66.1% | 50.7% / 60.7% |
| 7 | 90.81% / 92.13% | 76.2% / 80.6% | 63.1% / 71.5% |

## Fix found in play

The network can hire past farmer + 13 hands (the encoder's limit, the most the teacher ever had); in seed 20005
seat 0 that raised in the encoder on step 291 and the agent passed for the rest of the game. Hires are now capped
at the encoder's limit (`src/kagml/net_player.py`). All results below are from the rerun with the cap
(`<data>/runs/full-netp1c`); the 9 games before the crash are not used.
