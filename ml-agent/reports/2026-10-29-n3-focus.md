# Round N3 (focus takeovers) and weighted training (2026-10-29)

**Answer:** both new models beat the best agent by about 2,500 a game at about 3 se. Weighting adds nothing over
plain training (+30, se 954). New best: **`net:board-p5:market:overflow:endmask:endwork`**, share 0.894. All of
board-p5's gain is our own bank; fifth-nflow's bank is unchanged.

## What ran
- **Source games:** `src-n3-focus`, best agent, 45 plan training seeds, seat 0 (copied to `raw/src-n3`).
- **Takeovers:** `takeover_labels.py --tag takeover-n3 --focus`, hours 8-18 on days 6-24 and hours 4/8/12/16 on days
  25-28. 5,850 segments, all kept, full check passed. Capacity check: 0 segments over 14 units.
- **Dataset:** source `l3-n3`, 66,510 turns; market labels built for the 5,850 new segments (25,047 old ones reused).
- **Training:** both models `--init board-p4 --market --epochs 4 --lr 2e-4`, 439,825 training turns. That is
  309,211 for board-p4 plus N3 plus Codex's l1end data, so p5 against p4 is not a test of N3 alone.
  - `board-p5`: plain.
  - `board-p5w`: `--w-disagree 3` (command loss ×3 where board-p4 disagrees with the label) and `--value-market`
    (non-zero market labels also weighted by clamp(units × price / 200, 1, 8)).
  - Validation command accuracy: p5 0.9199, p5w 0.9197, p4 0.9143.
- **Tests:** 48 games each against live fifth-nflow, seeds 20001-20024, both seats, 5 workers.
- Logs: `<data>/runs/night4*.log`, `train-board-p5*.log`, `nflow-p5-best`, `nflow-p5w-best`.
