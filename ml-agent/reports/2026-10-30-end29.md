## Rule
Option `end29` in `src/kagml/net_player.py` (commit cbf81ce). Day 29 only:
- Goods an open shop buys are sold only on the turn right after a town pull (hours 1, 5, 9, 13, 17, 21). The stock is
  split evenly over the pulls left, and whatever is left goes at hour 22.
- Stock = max(shed, the template's own sell amount), because workers drop goods in the shed before the market runs.
- Goods no open shop buys keep the template's orders. If the shed holds more than 80, the template's orders are kept.
- Days 0-28 replay exactly as the baseline (seed 20001 checked frame by frame), so the paired difference is day 29 alone.

Script: scratchpad `d29.py` on `runs/sell-split` (teacher → board-p5, the 48 games of the split).

The rule got about 110 of the 594. The market is so oversupplied by day 29 that the shop pulls (1-2 units a shop
every 4 hours) barely lift the price. We sell 11 wool and 22 milk on day 29 into that market; fifth-nflow sells 5 and 12.

## Lessons
- The "loss against fifth-nflow's mean price" overstates what our own timing can recover. It includes fifth-nflow's own
  better prices and the market state we inherit. Before building the days 20-28 price floor (the 4.2k crashed-sales
  part), split it the same way.
- What is left is upstream: too much milk, wool and fertilizer late in the game, and markets already crashed by days 20-28.
