## Reading the table

- **Most of the remaining gap is macro**: the crop mix (tomatoes, early strawberries, endgame carrots), the herd
  (geese), and the market pressure on Metav4. Together these explain the days 12-17 surge and most of the
  37,000 margin gap. In our agent the template still makes these decisions, except one: **which seed to plant is
  chosen by the ranker, and v3-fw drifted away from tomatoes** (item 2), a side effect worth fixing on its own.
- **The micro gap has roughly halved** (one-day: -757 for v2, -408 for v3-diag). What remains: crops left dry on
  production nights, a little less fertilizer, extra shed trips, and no learned workers on days 0-5 and 29.
- **Item 6 is the least understood and possibly the largest**: Metav4 loses about 17,000 when it plays the
  teacher instead of us. Breaking down how (which products, which days, prices) is the next measurement if you
  want to target it.
