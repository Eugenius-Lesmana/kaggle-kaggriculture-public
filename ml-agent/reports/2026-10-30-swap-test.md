# Swap test: market or workers? (2026-10-30)

**Answer:** neither side alone. The teacher's market with our workers (MT) and the teacher's workers with our market (WT)
each recover part of the gap, and the two parts add up to roughly the whole teacher day (T). The market's apparent lead
comes from one game-day (20042 day 6, +50k). Without it, and on days 10-26, the worker side is the larger and steadier
part.

## Setup
- Tool: `tools/swap_test.py` (commit 6d4688d). Run: `runs/swap-p5-48`, 6,713 s on 8 workers.
- 48 board-p5 eval games (`raw/src-p5-eval`, seeds 20001-20048, seat 0) × days 6, 10, 14, 18, 22, 26 = 288 game-days.
- Each branch plays one day from the recorded morning against the opponent tape. A fresh teacher then plays every
  branch to the end. Values are final margin minus our branch (B). 0 teacher fallbacks.
- Caveat: the teacher plans workers and market together, so mixing them is approximate.

- Game 20042 day 6: T +51,430, MT +50,326, WT +145. Without it: T about +316, MT about +168, WT about +199.
- By day:
  - MT is large only on day 6 (+1,616, se 1,065), and negative on days 22 (−471) and 26 (−68). Our late market rules
    fit our own workers better than the teacher's late orders do.
  - WT is positive on days 10-26, with its biggest gain on day 18 (+671).
- Order differences (rest of the day):
  - with the teacher's market, we sell less of everything (fertilizer −2.7, strawberry −1.9, milk −1.1, wool −1.0)
    and buy fewer wheat products (−2.3);
  - with the teacher's workers there is more CARE (+0.7) and WATER (+0.5), and less PASS (−0.4).

## Addendum: game 20042 day 6 (same day)
Script: scratchpad `g20042.py`. It replays our day (B) and the teacher-market day (MT), then plays to the end.
- **The orders barely differ.** The day-6 dawn states are almost the same:
  - cash 94 against 151; strawberry 25 against 24, melon 13 against 12; shed 3 against 0; one more empty tile in MT.
  - animals and units are the same.
- **The difference grows in the playout:**
  - final banks: B 123,614 against 114,409; MT 147,703 against 93,692. The margin gap is +44.8k; the swap run had +50.3k,
    so reruns are not exactly repeatable.
  - Half of it is the opponent's bank (−20.7k). The opponent replays its tape open-loop, so the shared market pays its
    fixed sales differently. Its bank splits from day 9 on.
- **Verdict:** chaotic amplification, not a market lesson. Drop this game-day.
