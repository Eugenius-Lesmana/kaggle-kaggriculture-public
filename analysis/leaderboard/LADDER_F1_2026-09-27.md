## 2. Where the money goes

- Sales totals are equal on average.
- **In the games we lose,** the opponent sells about 340 more wheat and spends about
  $13k more. Harvest, feeding and herd size are identical. They **buy and resell
  wheat**: F1's tape schedules wheat round trips on the 4-hourly shop drain, and they
  run them bigger.
- The cash gap opens late: the median is −$37 on day 20, −$364 on day 28.

**Example** (episode 113883512, step 327): the tape buys 63 wheat and sells at 329.
- The fork bought 63 and **sold 70 at 328**, at the lifted price of 44.
- We bought 32 (trimmed by a layer I could not isolate; we had $18.7k cash) and sold
  23 at 329, at 41.

**The main pattern** (`.cache/fork_diffs.py`, 31 lost games against F1/F2 forks): the
forks **sell milk, strawberry and wool at steps where F1 doesn't** (24 of 31 games,
days 12–27), most often **one step before our next sale** of the same product.

That is F1's `MODELPX` lead seller, loosened. In F1 it only acts in hours 12–22 and
sells at most 3 units. F4 (the 27 Sept public successor) is F1 plus a series of exactly
these loosenings: caps of 6–10, more hours.

## 4. But maximum selling costs money against non-racers

`evaluation/mpx_guard/`, seeds 6521–6560:

| opponent | F1 + counter | + max selling | wins lost |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Metav4 | 76–4 | 55–25 | 23 |
| More Wheat | 80–0 | 67–13 | 13 |
| F2 | 66–14 | 27–53 | 39 |
| Farming Score | 78–2 | 75–5 | 3 |

**Max selling is rejected.**

An adaptive switch (go aggressive only when the rival pre-empts us) was attempted.
Three detector definitions produced the same number of events against racers and
non-racers alike (`.cache/rd_measure.py`). Racers sell whenever they predict a price
fall, not at a fixed offset. **Dropped.**

## 5. Recommendation

On our live opponent mix (54% F1-type, 18% F2, 12% Metav4, 14% other):
- **F4 + counter** is robust to the racing forks (65–15 and 60–20 against the
  aggressive stand-ins).
- It keeps most of the edge against the older families (66–80 of 80).

Weighted with the measured rates, it is about 82% against about 69% for F1 + counter.
**Submit F4 + counter, keep F1 + counter selected,** and re-check the ladder with
`fetch_episodes.py` after ~50 games.

independent pairing), 6601–6603 (detector diagnostics).
