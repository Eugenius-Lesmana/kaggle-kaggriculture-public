## 1. Why F4 + counter stalls at ~2220

Data: submission 56620927, 153 public games, fetched with
`research/tools/fetch_episodes.py` into `research/intake/replays/28 Sept f4/`.

- Overall 93–58–2. Against opponents rated 2300+: **5–18**.
- 102 of 153 games were decided by less than $1k.
- In most games both banks are identical until day 16. The gap opens from day 20.

**Opponents**, classified by replaying their observations through candidate agents
(`research/tools/reproduce.py`, `evaluation/census/reproduce_f4_ladder.json`):

| opponent | games | W–L–T | losses ≥ $1k |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **close F4 fork** (≥ 99% same worker moves) | 41 | **15–25–1** | 2 |
| looser F4 fork (50–99%) | 12 | 7–5 | 2 |
| exact F4 | 22 | 21–0–1 | 0 |
| other families (< 50% match) | 20 | 10–10 | 9 |
| F1 forks (exact, close, fork, partial) | 28 | 20–8 | 4 |
| F2 forks | 15 | 10–5 | 3 |
| Metav4 forks | 9 | 6–3 | 2 |
| More Wheat / ice-and-fire forks | 6 | 4–2 | 0 |

- **Close F4 forks are the problem.** They are 10 of the 18 losses against 2300+
  opponents.
- A fork plays F4 exactly, then deviates at one or two steps. The first
  deviation is almost always a sale made earlier or larger than F4's tape makes it.
  Examples:
  - step 576: 20–22 strawberries where F4 sells 10 now and 10–12 next turn;
  - step 150: 6 wool instead of 4;
  - step 327: 63 wheat instead of at 328;
  - step 340: 6 milk instead of 3.
- Different teams share signatures (step 576 ×4, step 150 ×4), so they copy
  common sources.
- In games where a fork's edit does not fire, it looks like exact F4, and the
  counter wins those.
- Scratch: `.cache/f4_firstdiff.py`, `.cache/f4_traj.py`.

**Copying the edits does not work.** F4's tape depends on the route chosen for
the map, so an edit keyed to a step rarely fires on other maps:
- single edits scored 1–1–78 (the only decided games were a baseline seat
  asymmetry);
- the union of 13 harvested edits scored **9–33–38** against the current
  submission.

Evidence: `evaluation/f4_race/E*_vs_cF4.json`, `U1_vs_cF4.json`.

## 2. The race fix

F4 already has a rule for selling early, `_s809_apply` (Step809). It sells now the
pure-cash units (strawberry, wool, egg, milk, melon, carrot, tomato) that the native
tape sells within the next 3 turns, but:
- only at quotes ≥ 50;
- never for the next turn's first sale.

`h1-counter-f4-race-candidate` removes both limits (2 lines). The counter is
unchanged.

A stand-in for the racing forks, F4 with the same two lines and no counter:
- matches the real forks **less** closely than plain F4 does;
- still reproduces the ladder result, beating the current submission 26 games in 80.

## 4. Reading

- The fix works against what beats us: F4-family mirrors and racing forks.
- It has no measured cost against the older families.
- The two fixes stack: the combination beats each one alone. On identical seeds it
  is equal or better than the current submission against every opponent tested.

**Limits:**
- The real ladder forks are more varied than one stand-in.
- The 2300+ "other family" opponents (different openers, $3k–30k wins) are
  untouched. No wrapper fixes those.
