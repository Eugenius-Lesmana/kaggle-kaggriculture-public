## Engine-derived edge

The engine executes market orders by order index, unit by unit. At each unit it
quotes both players against the **same pre-commit inventory**, then commits
both units (`kaggriculture.py`, `_process_market`). P1 does not get a better
same-index quote merely because it commits first. However, changing the order
of *different products* across indices changes which same-product batches
overlap; the market price curve can then make one alignment better for our
margin.

Metav4's RACE layer detects a clone from matching positions and route
similarity, reserves sales earlier, and escalates its horizon after a detected
lost race. Its step-1 equal-cash mirror setting changes a nominal 8- versus
24-turn horizon. This is **not** a usable edge here: the later V9 reservation
layer already applies a 40–48-turn item horizon to every RACE product. We
discarded the cash-mismatch idea without a reserved-seed screen.

The actual edge is in V44Y. `_v44y_reorder` calls
`_v44y_factor_margin(opp=orders, ...)`, taking its **unreordered** input as the
clone's orders, then returns a best-response permutation. A true clone also
returns the *reordered* result. Both copies make the same first-pass choice,
so the hypothetical opponent used for that choice is wrong. The appended
counter takes Metav4's finished action as its opponent hypothesis and
best-responds once more. It only permutes SELL orders within contiguous blocks
of length 2–6; it never changes products, quantities, purchases, or worker
actions. Its clone gate requires an active RACE clone state, matching banks and
worker positions, and route similarity at least 0.99. The gate latches because
our first successful sale can itself break bank equality.

On unreserved diagnostic seed 5901, three eligible turns had a combined
simulated relative-margin advantage of $97; the real game produced a $97
relative-bank swing against substrate self-play. That is a mechanism check,
not a performance estimate. The three changed sale-order blocks and final
banks in both seats are recorded in `evaluation/h1_counter/mechanism_5901.json`.

treated as independent strategy families. A positive result here does not
establish an advantage over the current leaderboard top teams.

**Reading:**
- The whole gain comes against **exact Metav4 copies**: +110 wins, none lost.
- Against the five other family members the counter rarely engages (the games are
  mostly bank-identical) and changes no result. Unmodified Metav4 already beats them
  115–118 of 120.
- The value on the ladder is therefore proportional to how often we meet an exact
  Metav4 copy. The census should measure that.

**Code review:** the overlay reuses Metav4's own V44Y block rule and margin model
(`_v44y_factor_margin`), with the mirror's *final* orders as the opponent hypothesis.
It changes only the order of SELLs. It is gated on the RACE clone state, equal banks,
equal positions and route similarity ≥ 0.99, and it falls back to the parent action on
any exception. No issues found. **Verdict: PASS, promote.** It is the best submission
candidate we have: it is never worse than Metav4 on the panel and strictly better
against Metav4 clones.
